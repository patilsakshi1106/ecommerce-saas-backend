New to Business Guide

Want to Start a Retail Brand, But Don't Know Where to Begin?

Follow our straightforward 4-step roadmap designed for first-time founders and new shop owners launching in India.

01

Niche & Margin Selection

Explore the external product categories in the top bar and shortlist high-margin Indian factories in Surat, Tiruppur, Jaipur, or Delhi NCR without broker commissions.

02

Low-MOQ Factory Sampling

Avoid locking up heavy capital in deadstock. Order manageable initial sample batches as a B2C Customer to test product quality and local customer demand first.

03

GST & Compliance Readiness

Once your product line is validated, register with your 15-digit GSTIN as a B2B Merchant to unlock wholesale slab pricing and claim full input tax credits.

04

Scaling & Reordering

Save 24%–32% on landed factory costs and reinvest those margins into custom packaging, fast restocks, and scaling your online or offline storefront.
